Consulting Services

Software & Data Architecture, and Digital / Tech Innovation Management

I work with engineering teams and organizations across two tracks, each available as a scoped engagement, a project-based build, or an ongoing fractional retainer.

Software & Data Architecture Consulting

For engineering teams and product owners who need a trusted architect for their software, data, and cloud systems — someone who has built and scaled these systems firsthand, not just reviewed them on paper.

Architecture Review & Audit

A scoped, time-boxed assessment of an existing system, data pipeline, or cloud setup. Deliverable: a findings report and a prioritized roadmap for reliability, scalability, and cost.

Architecture Design & Build

A project-based engagement to design and implement a system, data pipeline, or cloud migration — from architecture decisions through to a working, production-ready solution.

Fractional Architect / Advisory Retainer

Ongoing, part-time architectural oversight for a team or product — design reviews, technical decision support, and mentoring for senior engineers.

Digital / Tech Innovation Management Consulting

For organizations trying to figure out what AI, automation, and no-code/low-code should actually mean for their business — and how to turn that into a working program, not just a slide deck.

Innovation Diagnostic / Workshop

A structured session (or series) to map out where AI, automation, and no-code/low-code adoption can realistically create value in your organization.

Innovation Program Design & Rollout

A project-based engagement to define and stand up an innovation initiative — for example an AI adoption roadmap or an internal tooling strategy — and see it through to launch.

Fractional Innovation Advisor

An ongoing retainer guiding tech strategy and innovation decisions as your organization's needs evolve.
